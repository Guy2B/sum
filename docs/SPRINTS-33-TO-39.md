# Sigma Sprints 33 to 39

This increment turns the shared platform foundations into a testable personal operating cycle.

## Sprint 33 — Goal & Outcome Engine
Canonical goals, weighted milestones, dependencies, progress, risk and review explanations.

## Sprint 34 — Task Execution Engine
Task lifecycle, dependency enforcement, capacity-aware deterministic selection and approval metadata.

## Sprint 35 — Decision Engine
Evidence-adjusted option ranking, explicit risk penalties, confidence, explanations and outcome learning.

## Sprint 36 — Attention Engine
Grouped alerts, severity ordering, cooldown control, snoozing, preferences and notification limits.

## Sprint 37 — Review & Reflection Engine
Daily, weekly or monthly review material with progress metrics, risks, blocked work and reflection prompts.

## Sprint 38 — Domain Adapter Framework
A strict reusable contract for normalization, validation, signals, insights, proposed actions and explanation. Proposed external actions remain approval-gated.

## Sprint 39 — Personal Operating System
An end-to-end orchestration path from goals and tasks through the existing planner to attention and review.

## Architectural guarantees
- Common engines remain domain-independent.
- Results are deterministic for equivalent inputs.
- Recommendations and actions remain explainable.
- External or consequential actions require human approval.
- Workspace boundaries remain explicit in canonical records.

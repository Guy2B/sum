- Sprint 50: UTF-8 integrity and mojibake prevention.
- Sprint 51: shared visual design tokens.
- Sprint 52: integrated product navigation shell.
- Sprint 53: usable goal management and progress.
- Sprint 54: visual task workflow.
- Sprint 55: interactive daily planning.
- Sprint 56: daily review experience.
- Sprint 57: versioned local persistence and merge.
- Sprint 58: portable JSON export/import.
